Folders

Folder names start at the left edge. Sites go on indented lines under their folder (Tab adds two spaces).
Name: site, site puts those sites in one group called Name. site, site gives each site its own group.
A site is a domain (google.com covers mail.google.com) or a piece of a URL with / or * (facebook.com/marketplace). The most specific match wins. Anything not listed goes in Unsorted.
