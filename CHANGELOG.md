# Changelog

## 2.0.0 (first public release)

- **Runs on any Mac:** one universal app for Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 Big Sur and later, Safari 15.4 and later.
- **Guided setup window:** opening the app shows each step to turn Tab Triage on in Safari, with a live tick when it's on.
- **Build it yourself with a free Apple ID:** `Build_and_Install.command` checks for Xcode and your certificate, explains anything missing, signs with your own team and installs into your Applications folder.
- **Prebuilt download** on the Releases page for a quick try (unsigned).
- **Worldwide starter folders** for new installs. Existing folders are never changed.
- **New icon.**
- **Usage** works when Safari shares its web processes with other apps (the numbers then include those apps).

## Before 2.0

Private versions, in short:

- **1.5:** Refresh stale (reload tabs unused for days) and Reset tabs (fresh start without quitting Safari).
- **1.4:** Saved sessions: save every open tab, restore all, one window or one page.
- **1.3:** Folder organizer with drag and drop. Double-click a tab to go to it.
- **1.2:** Usage: Safari's memory and CPU, and what a close freed.
- **1.1:** Folders above groups.
- **1.0:** Every tab grouped by site; check, close in one batch, one-minute undo.
