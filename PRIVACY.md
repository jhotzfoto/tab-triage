# Privacy

Tab Triage keeps everything on your Mac. It has no servers, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

## What it reads

- **Each tab's title and address**, to sort your tabs into folders. Safari only shows an extension this on websites you allow it on, which is why it asks for access to all websites.
- **Nothing inside web pages.** Tab Triage has no content scripts: it can't see what's on a page, what you type or your passwords.
- **Memory and CPU numbers**, only when you press **Usage**: Safari's and the Mac's totals. Tab titles and addresses are never sent to this part.

## What it stores, on your Mac only

Inside Safari's storage for Tab Triage:

- Your folders and settings.
- Saved sessions (the titles and addresses of the tabs you chose to save).
- For one minute after a close, the closed tabs' addresses, so Undo can reopen them.
- When each tab was last used, so **Refresh stale** knows which tabs are old.

**Private tabs are never stored**, not even for Undo.

Removing Tab Triage from Safari removes all of this.
